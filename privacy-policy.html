Privacy Policy

Tiny Paws Rescue: Puzzle · Developer: faisal404 · faisal404-lab.github.io · Last updated: 27 October 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Tiny Paws Rescue: Puzzle ("the game", "we") uses, why, and the choices you have.

Information we do not collect

The game has no sign-up or account. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location, and we do not run our own servers that receive your data.

Game progress

Your level, coins, power-ups and rescued animals are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes this progress.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads using Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process device information such as the advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system and how you interact with ads. In the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area and Switzerland you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings › Google › Ads). Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases (Google Play)

In-app purchases and the Paws Club subscription are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your card or payment details. Google tells the game only which items you own so they can be delivered and restored. You can manage or cancel subscriptions at any time in the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Advertising and purchase data is handled by Google under its own security practices. Game progress stays on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
